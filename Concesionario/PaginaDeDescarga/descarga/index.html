Service & Maintenance

Your car's maintenance schedule, on your own computer.

Not on ours. The app installs on your machine, keeps your vehicle's record in a folder you pick, and works with the network off.

Install it

Open the app

It opens in your browser and offers to install itself. The first time it asks you for a folder — that folder is where your record lives, and it is yours.

What it keeps

  • Every scheduled operation and where it stands.
  • Why it stands there, in the schedule's own words.
  • Your odometer readings, your deferrals, your usage profile.

Who can read it

  • You, from the app.
  • Your own AI assistant, which reads that folder directly.
  • Nobody else. The record never leaves your machine.

What it does not do

It does not ask us anything about you, and it cannot. The app reads one thing from us — the service events for your vehicle — with a credential scoped to exactly that. Anything else we hold about you answers 403 to this app, by construction rather than by promise.

Sotaventos Motors is a fictional dealership. This page and the app it links to are a working prototype.
