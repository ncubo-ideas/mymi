It works with Mymi.

Mymi is the app that gathers what each institution hands you —your lab, your supermarket— and shows it to your AI assistant, on your own device. You install it once, for free; after that, each institution is added with one tap. Sotavento Motors is one of them.

On your Android phone

Mymi is on Google Play. Sotavento Motors is added from this very page, with one tap.

Get it onGoogle Play Add Sotavento Motors to Mymi

  1. Install Mymi from Google Play. It is free and you install it once: the same Mymi serves every institution you have.

  2. Come back to this page on your phone and tap “Add Sotavento Motors to Mymi”. Mymi downloads the module from this same address and shows you a sheet: who hands it over, what questions it will answer and in which folder of the phone it keeps your vehicle’s record. Nothing is written before you read it.

  3. Tap “Install on my phone”. Sotavento Motors appears in your list of institutions.

  4. Ask. Your assistant sees Sotavento Motors next to your other institutions. Every query has a switch: you turn off the ones you do not want it to read.

Reading this on a computer? In Mymi tap Paste their link and paste this one. If the dealership.gmod file reached you by mail or WhatsApp, open it: the phone offers Mymi. https://go.gnothi.app/alta?bundle=…/descargas/movil/dealership.gmod

On your computer

Mymi for Windows downloads from here. It asks for no administrator rights and touches nothing outside its folder.

Download forWindows Download the Sotavento Motors module

  1. Download and install Mymi. Once. If Windows shows the SmartScreen notice, choose “More info” and “Run anyway”. The first time it opens, Mymi asks before registering with your assistant (Claude or another) and backs up the configuration it is about to touch.

  2. Open Mymi and choose “Add an institution”. Paste the Sotavento Motors link below, or tap “Pick the file…” and find the dealership.gmod you downloaded with the button.

  3. Read the sheet and tap “Install on this computer”. It tells you who hands over the module, what it will answer and in which folder it keeps your vehicle’s record: Documents\Gnothi\dealership. The same one the app uses.

  4. Restart your assistant and ask. “Is my car still under warranty?” Everything is answered from your computer; nothing goes to the cloud.

The Sotavento Motors link, to paste into Mymi: …/descargas/dealership.gmod

No Mymi? The app also works on its own, in the browser: open it and pick the folder where you want your vehicle’s record kept. When you install Mymi, it reads that same folder.

Without Mymi: open it on its own

Open the app

It opens in your browser and offers to install itself. The first time it asks you for a folder — that folder is where your record lives, and it is yours.

What it keeps

  • Every scheduled operation and where it stands.
  • Why it stands there, in the schedule's own words.
  • Your odometer readings, your deferrals, your usage profile.

Who can read it

  • You, from the app.
  • Your own AI assistant, which reads that folder directly.
  • Nobody else. The record never leaves your machine.

What it does not do

It does not ask us anything about you, and it cannot. The app reads one thing from us — the service events for your vehicle — with a credential scoped to exactly that. Anything else we hold about you answers 403 to this app, by construction rather than by promise.

Sotavento Motors is a fictional dealership. This page and the app it links to are a working prototype.
